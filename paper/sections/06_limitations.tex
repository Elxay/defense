\section{Limitations}
The evidence concerns one primary steering model and one supplementary prompt/filter model. There is no trained Qwen steering vector, and an independent PAIR test campaign was not run. WJB supplies a different attack family but does not substitute for that missing test. Val70 is success-selected and reused for alpha matching; it is not an independent final test.

Our harmfulness judge is an operational model classifier. Cross-node and backend agreement do not establish validity on WJB, non-English outputs, or unusual response formats. Refusal categories and task-completion labels are model annotations without independent human calibration. A planned ten-item blind reread from a 51-item audit remains incomplete. Unread negative outputs and token-limited continuations prevent interpreting reviewed ranges as statistical truth bounds.

The benign wrapper samples share a dataset source, and the augmentation training set and held-out pairs are from WildJailbreak. Exact and full-window embedding audits reduce obvious overlap but do not establish semantic independence below cosine 0.85. The original two-arm intervention confounds wrapped data with additional benign data and keep reweighting. The plain-data supplement has been expanded to five seeds, remains limited in precision, and is not content-matched. Any interpolation assumes local linearity in a noisy 70-item ASR axis.

Five training seeds and four random directions give limited estimates of their populations. Shared prompts, repeated vectors, post-hoc-selected seed0 analyses, and amendments after earlier observations restrict confirmatory interpretation. The historical-vector and three-rerun comparison does not partition training and seed variance. A second seed3 control is being evaluated on the full holdout to contextualize the 2-pp criterion; a single observed repeat cannot validate that criterion generally. We preserve failed and superseded criteria and report dates of effect reads. No ongoing augmentation outcome is presented as complete evidence.
