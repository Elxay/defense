\section{Limitations}
The evidence concerns one primary steering model and one supplementary prompt/filter model. There is no trained Qwen steering vector. The completed PAIR-style campaign provides a new fixed prompt set, with all 500 source rows retained, but most behaviors have historical training or selection exposure. The 228 untouched rows are reported separately by functional stratum; they do not turn the full sample into a behavior-independent test. Attacks were optimized against the undefended Llama model rather than each defense. Val70 is success-selected and reused for alpha matching; it is not an independent final test.

Our harmfulness judge is an operational model classifier. Cross-node and backend agreement do not establish validity on WildJailbreak, non-English outputs, or unusual response formats. Refusal categories and task-completion labels are model annotations without independent human calibration. A single-reader audit of 51 selected flips gave union-rule precision of 24/24 and recall of 24/41 (about 0.59) against its labels. Recall may vary with response style; these counts do not calibrate the rule for other conditions. A planned ten-item blind reread remains incomplete. Unread negative outputs and token-limited continuations prevent interpreting reviewed ranges as statistical truth bounds.

The benign wrapper samples share a dataset source, and the augmentation training set and held-out pairs are from WildJailbreak. Exact and full-window embedding audits reduce obvious overlap but do not establish semantic independence below cosine 0.85. The original two-arm intervention confounds wrapped data with additional benign data and keep reweighting. The data-composition experiment and its plain-data supplement were closed incomplete and do not contribute a main-text intervention result. The supplement was not content-matched. Any interpolation assumes local linearity in a noisy 70-item ASR axis.

Five training seeds and four random directions give limited estimates of their populations. The 10-pp advantage threshold was set in investigator discussion without an empirical basis for practical importance. Its attainment is sensitive to the random reference: 3/5 under the specified two directions and 5/5 under the supplementary four. Shared prompts, repeated vectors, post-hoc-selected seed0 analyses, and amendments after earlier observations restrict confirmatory interpretation. The historical-vector and three-rerun comparison does not partition training and seed variance. A proposed second seed3 holdout control does not supply a completed noise-floor estimate; a single observed repeat would not validate that criterion generally. We preserve failed and superseded criteria and report dates of effect reads. No ongoing augmentation outcome is presented as complete evidence.

The frozen rule-scored PAIR-style prompts are optimized against undefended Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct. Their evaluation under steering and system prompts measures transfer for this attack instance on HarmBench and JBB-Behaviors. Adaptive attacks against the individual defended conditions require separate evaluation. HarmBench copyright outcomes measure MinHash matches. Classifier-based harmfulness and copyright matches are interpreted by functional stratum.

The twenty-row zero-vector cap-proxy check contains no cap-positive case, so it cannot estimate the proxy's sensitivity. Later conditions with raw generated-token metadata can support a separately identified calibration. The earlier zero-restart snapshot concerned the PAIR recovery watchdog at that time; a later recorded OOM restart and the undocumented third-arm seed3 restart are distinct events.

\paragraph{Official evaluation and public refusal costs.}
The official WildJailbreak prompts were selected for success against at least one weak model, and source behaviors can overlap historical training or selection. HarmBench requires an unvalidated nearest-neighbor behavior mapping, whereas WildGuard shares the WildJailbreak training source; neither provides human ground truth. Public refusal comparisons use a matched batch-8 path and120-token replies, while harmful-side generation is single-row with2400-token replies. Refusal-style and truncation effects can change both regular-expression and model labels. The six fixed30-pair model samples contain no clear refusal misses but retain uncertain outputs; the benign WildJailbreak random sample fails the conservative line with five uncertain items. They therefore do not validate absolute recall.

\paragraph{Missing labels and model ports.}
PAIR-style WildGuard scoring retains field-specific INVALID rows rather than treating them as safe. The pooled nine-unit complete-case comparison uses 397 of 400 harmfulness-applicable rows; the full-denominator identification bounds are reported separately. Complete-case intervals do not cover uncertainty about why labels are missing. Crossing WildGuard's 4096-token sliding window is logged and is not itself proof of invalidity. For pending model ports, the native donor-target gate and its subsequent replacement by uniform fixed Llama targets were decided after the Qwen baseline was known but before new-model vector training. The original failed gate is preserved. Llama-style target wording may differ from a new model's natural refusals and may favor the v2 rule; WildGuard refusal is reported alongside it. The fixed target cache is a reconstruction, not a certificate that every historical Llama seed used byte-identical targets. No pending port or target-transfer result is counted as a completed model replication.

\paragraph{Fixed-budget Qwen optimization.}
The unified Qwen intervention uses 40 optimization steps and fixed Llama-style refusal targets. Its final attack loss remains approximately 1.8--2.1, with no convergence established under this budget. Target transfer, model-specific loss scales, and incomplete optimization are confounded with failure to reach the calibration refusal target; the calibration result does not identify one cause. We retain the same step budget across models and report individual seeds. A longer-training variant is not part of the main round. WildGuard refusal is the calibration reference, while v2 is a secondary rule-based measure rather than human ground truth.
