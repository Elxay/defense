The augmentation setup used same-pipeline retrained controls and matched val70 ASR. Treatment seeds 3 and 5 use $\alpha=1.5$, hence norm 7.815, while their controls use norm 5.21. This is attack-ASR matching at different intervention strengths, not a fixed-strength comparison. A fixed-strength supplement for these two seeds was specified before the matched held-out effects were read; it does not replace matching. The target is one control run; its observed retraining spread does not permit interpreting fine differences within the 5-pp tolerance as precise matching. The first matching results are in Table~\ref{tab:matching}; the third-arm held-out evaluation was closed as incomplete, and no style-specific augmentation conclusion is reported. Adding wrapped benign examples also changes benign-data volume and halves each old keep weight. A separate unwrapped-data arm was initially specified on seeds 3--5 and then expanded to all five seeds before its holdout evaluation, for greater precision. Without that arm, the intervention can only be described as augmentation with 120 benign inputs that include wrappers, not a pure style-matching effect.
