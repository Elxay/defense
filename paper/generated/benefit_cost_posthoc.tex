The unpaired harmful-minus-benign refusal-rate gap within the WildJailbreak evaluation data is 30.37 pp at zero, 34.36 pp for random directions, and 54.76 pp for learned vectors. These differently constructed and sized sets are not paired, so the gap is descriptive and is not a selectivity or mechanism estimate. The cross-dataset pattern is compatible with packaging-related response differences but does not test that explanation. The matched vanilla--wrapped experiments in Table~\ref{tab:c2} provide a separate paired comparison.

On XSTest unsafe, rule-defined refusal capability does not improve. A targeted model reread of49 baseline-positive to learned-negative pairs over19 prompts finds two safety redirections,21 premise-rebuttal boundary cases,20 other content responses, five cautioned responses and one knowledge nonanswer. Counting premise rebuttals as refusal-with-alternative yields23; excluding them yields two. This shared-context,120-token review is not human calibration and does not establish an increase in harmful compliance.
