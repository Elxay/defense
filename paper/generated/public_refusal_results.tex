The harmful-side advantage does not imply uniform benign costs. The frozen v2 comparison gives -1.20, +3.95, and +9.05 pp on XSTest safe, OR-Bench-Hard, and official WildJailbreak benign prompts, respectively. Full and keep60-unseen layers are shown together; the training overlap is retained, not removed from the main result. WildGuard refusal sensitivity has the same signs, but it sees the same 120-token truncated outputs and is not human ground truth.

In six fixed 30-pair model reviews, no response was read as a clear task refusal or refusal with alternative. The benign WildJailbreak random group has five uncertain responses and fails the conservative rule that treats uncertainty as a miss. Thus these samples do not validate absolute refusal accuracy. The samples repeat some prompts across vector conditions, use shared conversation context, and lack independent human calibration.

On the separately reported 200-row unsafe XSTest control, the v2 refusal difference is -3.90 pp; this refusal-capability reading is not an attack-success estimate.
